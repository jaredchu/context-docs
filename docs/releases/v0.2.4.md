# v0.2.4: clarity without losing meaning

**Experimental prerelease, October 17, 2026.**
Bundle: **context-docs 0.2.4**, **adopt-context-docs 0.1.13** and unchanged
**standard 0.1.1**. Previous bundle: [v0.2.3](v0.2.3.md).

## What changes

Both skills prefer clear, direct sentences and consistent project terminology
within authorized context edits. Split crowded sentences while preserving
conditions, uncertainty, rationale and evidence.

- Keep conditions attached to their actions: a passing check can permit asking
  for approval without granting that approval.
- Keep unknowns accurate: missing evidence is not a failed result, and an
  unrecorded actor remains unknown.
- Preserve decisions, their reasons and source links. Clearer wording must not
  strengthen the evidence or turn a proposal into an approved decision.
- Preserve existing layouts and unrelated owner text. The preference does not
  authorize broad copyediting or require ASD-STE100 compliance, a controlled
  dictionary, sentence limits or a new template.

The [core skill](../../skills/context-docs/SKILL.md#make-the-smallest-useful-update)
owns the preference; [adoption](../../skills/adopt-context-docs/SKILL.md) follows it
within its existing scope. Concise Markdown progress and everyday maintenance
continue as before; no new invocation is required for the writing preference.

## Existing projects and upgrades

Installing or upgrading remains a separate action. Follow the
[upgrade guide](../getting-started.md#upgrading), keeping both complete sibling
packages and preserving local customizations. Publication does not upgrade
installed skills or rewrite project setups.

Existing context paths, equivalent rules, adoption dates, owner decisions, layouts,
local-only boundaries, logging settings and saved studies stay in place.
Audits remain read-only. The preference adds no runtime, background tracking or
new storage format.

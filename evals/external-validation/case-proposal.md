You may submit links and short answers; the coordinator handles technical setup.
Choose the event and task before exploring this project's skill or results where
possible. Do not read existing cases simply to imitate their expected outputs.
Submit through the [packet's issue/PR route](README.md#two-ways-to-contribute).
This **entire proposal is evaluator-only**. Subjects receive only the frozen task,
write boundary, pinned corpus and declared treatment, not this completed form.

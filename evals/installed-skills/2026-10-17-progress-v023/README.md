work and original adoption evidence, remain equal. Earlier dated observations and
review history stay in maintenance evidence beside the new result. All 32 installed
package files, project setup rules, source packages, saved studies and prior private
evidence are compared against the protected pre-run byte/mode inventory. Git metadata
and routine native-client global bookkeeping are outside that inventory; no global

These are guided, author-scored cases using the familiar two-check fixture.

They do not establish implicit discovery, fresh setup, cross-session continuity,
independent validation, general reliability, usability or user benefit. Sparse,
local-only, audit/repeat and explicitly requested extra-detail branches and the
full progress suite remain unrun. Earlier studies and reviews remain unchanged.
No skill changes, infrastructure, release, deployment or installed upgrade follow
from this smoke.

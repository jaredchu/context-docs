# Record identity — owner Mira, September 15
Use the exact pair (tenant,id) as the identity. Two tenants may independently
use the same id; deduplicating globally by id would drop one tenant's job.
Keep the first occurrence of each pair, including its payload. IDs and tenant
names are case-sensitive strings; do not normalize them. These semantics apply
to the existing formatter and all preview features. The source file is evidence
and must not be overwritten by formatting operations.

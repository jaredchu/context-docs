The curated final answer only says "Mira's October 4 reply replaced it" (`curated-final.txt:5`). It never says why: that downstream consumers treat arrival order as priority. "No incremental correctness… advantage… both succeed" (`assessment.json:3`) hides the one difference between the arms. On this criterion the curated arm did slightly worse, not equally well. The conclusion should say so rather than call the arms tied.

**3. The verifier-freeze claim is worded too loosely and can't be checked.**

So the verifier was frozen after the curated arm had finished and its outputs existed, not just "after subject launch" (`assessment.json:138`). "Before inspecting subject outputs" can't be confirmed from anything here. The verifier only shows the expected outputs, so this probably didn't affect scores. It's still a confound between the arms that the write-up doesn't disclose.

**4. The speed comparison is confounded.**
"Curated was slower here" (42.5 s vs 27.2 s) is accurate for this one run. But the order was fixed (curated first), the prompts differ (curated has an extra routing sentence and one more document to read), and curated's final turn included about 5.3 s of thinking. The assessment does avoid generalizing, but it should name these confounds next to the timing.
